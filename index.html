Questions

Will it make up wrong answers about my business?

No. It's instructed to answer only from the business info you provide. If a customer asks something it doesn't know, it says so and offers to take their contact details for you.

How do bookings and orders reach me?

Instantly by email — the moment a visitor books or orders, you get an email with all the details. Everything is also saved to your owner dashboard.

What about no-shows?

Turn on appointment reminders and customers automatically get an email 24 hours before their booking. You get a daily digest of who's coming in tomorrow. It runs on its own — no extra work.

Can it handle my business hours?

Yes. Set your open days, hours, and max bookings per day — and mark vacation or holiday dates as closed in your dashboard. Customers book from a real calendar where closed and fully-booked days are greyed out, so the bot never accepts a booking you can't honor. They can even just ask — "is Saturday full?" — and the AI checks your live schedule and suggests the nearest open day.

Does it get smarter over time?

Yes. Every question it can't answer lands in your dashboard. You type the answer once, and it knows it forever — trained by your real customers.

Can customers reschedule their own bookings?

Yes. They ask in the chat, verify with their phone number, and pick a new time or cancel — no phone tag. You get notified of every change.

I'm a realtor — what does it do for me?

Realtor mode greets visitors with "Looking for a home?", answers listing questions from your info, captures every buyer and seller inquiry as a lead (emailed to you instantly), books showings, and reminds buyers the day before. It's a full-time assistant for your listings.

Can I add photos?

Yes. Drag & drop photos into your dashboard — from a PC or your phone. Label each one ("123 Main St — exterior"), and the AI will offer to show them when customers ask about something matching, right in the chat.

Do reminders work by text message?

Yes. Appointment reminders go out as texts to the customer's phone (with email as backup), and you can get your daily digest by text too. It runs on Twilio — about a penny per message.

How do deposits work?

Set an amount in your dashboard and paste a Stripe payment link (takes about 2 minutes to create in Stripe). After booking, the customer sees a "Pay deposit" button. No Stripe? Add a Cash App or Zelle note instead — or leave deposits off entirely.

What happens when a day is fully booked?

The bot offers a one-tap waitlist. When someone cancels, the next person waiting is texted automatically — cancelled slots refill themselves.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. It auto-detects the visitor's language — Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Chinese and more — and replies in it, translating your business info on the fly. You write everything once, in English.

Does it work with my website builder?

Yes — WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, or plain HTML. If you can paste a line of code (or have someone who can), it works.

What happens when I cancel?

The chatbot is removed and billing stops. No lock-in, no fees.

Can I change my business info later?

Anytime. Update your hours, menu, or prices and the chatbot picks it up immediately.
